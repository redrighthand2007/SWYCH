# Swych Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

> Host this page at a public URL (for example GitHub Pages) and add the link in the Play
> Console and the app's store listing before publishing.

Swych ("we", "the app") is a marketplace for students of VIT Vellore to exchange items
with each other. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Name and hostel block | Shown to other students on your listings and deals |
| College email address | Account login; restricts sign-up to students |
| Phone number | Login by phone, and shared with the other student **only after** a deal is accepted so you can arrange the hand-over |
| Password | Stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see it |
| Item listings (title, description, price, category, photo) | Shown to other signed-in students |
| Offers and deals (prices, status, confirmations) | Visible only to the two students in the deal |
| Reputation counts (completed and cancelled deals) | Shown on your profile and listings |
| App settings (theme, haptics, notifications) | Stored only on your device |
| Deal notification state | The app checks your deals in the background (about every 15 minutes) and remembers on your device which updates it has already shown you. You can turn this off in Profile → Deal Notifications. |

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or payment details, and we do not
show ads.

## How information is shared

- **Other students** see your name, block, reputation and listings. Your phone number is
  shown only to the other side of a deal you have accepted or completed.
- **Service providers**: data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (database,
  authentication and photo storage). Item photos are stored in a public bucket so they can
  be displayed in the app.
- We do not sell your information.

## Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Delete account**. This
permanently removes your profile, listings and deals. Photos you uploaded may remain in
storage until removed manually; email us to have them deleted.

To request deletion without the app, email whiterighthand2007@gmail.com.

## Security

Access to data is restricted by database rules so that students can only change their own
listings and deals. No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable
measures to protect your information.

## Children

Swych is intended for college students aged 18 and over.

## Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes and note the date above.
